Corrections to Circulating Lists

Versions of this victim list circulate publicly, in Gig Workers Rising's database and in viral social media compilations. Every case on this page was verified independently against news reports, police statements, prosecutors' offices, and court records. This section corrects the errors in those circulating versions; the compilations themselves are not a source for any fact in this dataset.
Errors CorrectedDate errors in the circulating lists:
Lists date the Howato attack January 26; Tempe police and court records place it about 1 AM on Sunday, January 27. Lists date the Gandonou stabbing March 3; he was attacked the night of Saturday, March 2, and the first major coverage ran the next day. Lists date the French killing September 20; Hampton police and court records place it about 11:50 AM on September 16, the same morning as the Newport News gas station murder committed by the same man. Lists date the Ozgur killing December 24; the Denny's robbery occurred about 2:25 AM on December 26, the morning after Christmas, per police and trial evidence.
SupportedThe institutional victim count for 2019 is supported.
Gig Workers Rising's database lists 8 app-based drivers killed on the job in 2019. This page's independently verified roster is also 8: no additional 2019 case surfaced in sweeps of rideshare and delivery coverage, and no listed case failed verification. The count reconciles exactly.
Partially SupportedCompilations code unnamed and unresolved suspects by inference. This dataset codes suspect race only where it can be verified. Of the 9 counted suspects in 2019, 8 have a verified race: 6 Black, 1 Hispanic, 1 White. The other 1 are left unverified rather than assigned a race.
Status UpdatesCompilation case statuses are frozen at compile time.
Since the circulating lists were compiled: Durazo pleaded guilty and was sentenced to 50 years (September 2022); Wilson pleaded guilty and was sentenced to 50 years (April 2021); Whitaker pleaded guilty to aggravated manslaughter and was sentenced to 10 years (October 2022), while the Rhoden shooter remains unidentified; Anderson was convicted at trial (September 2022) and sentenced to 87 years (June 2023); and Walker pleaded guilty (January 2024) and received 17 years to serve. The Fanelli, Evans, and Armstrong murder cases showed no public adjudication as of August 2026.
Methodology: Victim list anchored to Gig Workers Rising's Murdered Behind the Wheel report and public database, the institutional list covering 2017 through April 2023, then independently verified case by case; every fact on this page cites local news, police statements, prosecutors' offices, or court records, and claims examined in the corrections section above come from viral social media compilations, which are not cited as a source for any fact. Inclusion criteria: homicide of a driver working for an app-based rideshare or delivery platform (Amazon Flex included) at the time of the killing, or killed in direct connection with that work. Killings while working but not because of the job are included motive-blind with circumstances stated, matching the institutional list's murdered-while-working standard; every 2019 case was killed in direct connection with the work, so no motive-blind edge case arises this year. Non-criminal traffic accidents are excluded. No police-involved killing of a driver was documented in 2019. The Howato case is counted as one victim; she was pregnant, her unborn daughter also died, and the defendant was convicted on two murder counts. The Tchakounte case was a double homicide; his co-passenger Casey Robinson was also killed and falls outside this driver dataset but is stated in the row. Bias-motive coding is omitted for this dataset. Suspect race comes from a booking, prison or court record, a published photo, or a news report, and the Race source column shows which; where the author judged race from a photo without a record it reads “Author call,” surnames are never treated as documentation, and anyone whose race could not be verified is left unverified. Relationship between killer and victim is coded documented-only: Passenger (rode on the fatal trip), Customer (delivery), Stranger with the mechanism noted, Known dispute, or Ex-partner where applicable; where the killer's relationship is unreported or the killer is unidentified in an otherwise resolved case it is coded Not documented, and unsolved cases always code Unknown regardless of any suspected mechanism. "Solved/Charged" counts any case with at least one murder-charged or murder-arrested suspect; the Rhoden case is counted as convicted on that basis through the getaway driver's aggravated manslaughter plea, with the shooter noted as unidentified and at large. All dates, ages, sentences, and statuses verified as of August 2026.
