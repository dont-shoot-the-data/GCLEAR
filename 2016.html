Corrections to Circulating Lists

Versions of this victim list circulate publicly in viral social media compilations and older archived tallies; no institutional list exists for 2016. Every case on this page was verified independently against news reports, police statements, prosecutors' offices, and court records. This section corrects the errors in those circulating versions, and the compilations themselves are not a source for any fact in this dataset.
Errors CorrectedDate and detail errors in the circulating lists:
Circulating lists date the Bayyan killing March 19; the shooting was reported just before 4 AM on Sunday, March 20. First-day reports gave Diagne's age as 50; Wayne County prosecutor records say 51. An early wire report placed the Diagne shooting just after 12 AM Saturday; the prosecutor places it after 11 PM on Saturday, March 19. Zolfaghari's age was reported as both 34 and 36; his published obituary and medical examiner reporting say 34.
Partially SupportedZolfaghari is carried on some lists as an Uber driver.
He drove for both Uber and Lyft. Atlanta police said he was on duty for Lyft on the fatal trip and subpoenaed Lyft's records, so this dataset codes him Lyft, with the dual-platform fact noted in his row.
Status UpdatesCompilation case statuses are frozen at compile time.
Since the circulating lists were compiled: Watson was convicted at trial in February 2017 and sentenced that March to life plus 37.5 to 75 years and a consecutive firearm term for killing Diagne. In the Zolfaghari case, Edward Lee Tucker was charged and jailed per July 2025 reporting, roughly nine years after the killing; no public verdict was found as of August 2026. The Bayyan case remains unsolved with no arrest found through August 2026.
Methodology: No institutional victim list exists for 2016: Gig Workers Rising's Murdered Behind the Wheel report and public database, the anchor for the 2017 through early-2023 pages of this project, begin in 2017 and do not cover this year. This dataset was compiled directly from local news reports, police statements, prosecutors' offices, and court records, and its totals should be read as floors. Claims examined in the corrections section above come from circulating lists and viral social media compilations; those compilations are not cited as a source for any fact. Inclusion criteria: homicide of a driver working for an app-based rideshare or delivery platform (Amazon Flex included) at the time of the killing, or killed in direct connection with that work. Killings while working but not because of the job are included motive-blind with circumstances stated; no 2016 case required that basis. Non-criminal traffic accidents are excluded. No police-involved killing of a driver was documented in 2016. Bias-motive coding is omitted for this dataset. Suspect race comes from a booking, prison or court record, a published photo, or a news report, and the Race source column shows which; where the author judged race from a photo without a record it reads “Author call,” surnames are never treated as documentation, and anyone whose race could not be verified is left unverified. Relationship between killer and victim is coded documented-only: Passenger (rode on or summoned the fatal trip), Customer (delivery recipient), Stranger with the mechanism noted, Known dispute, or Ex-partner where applicable; where a suspect is charged but the relationship is unreported it is coded Not documented, and unsolved cases always code Unknown regardless of any suspected mechanism. "Solved/Charged" counts any case with at least one murder-charged or murder-arrested suspect. All dates, ages, sentences, and statuses verified as of August 2026.
