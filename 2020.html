Combined analysis and case-by-case dataset. Data from local news, police statements, prosecutors' offices, and court records, verified through August 2026.
Victim list anchored to Gig Workers Rising's Murdered Behind the Wheel report and database, the institutional list covering 2017 through April 2023, independently verified and expanded; every fact below cites local news, police statements, prosecutors, or court records.
This dataset covers 7 victims, all homicides. No police-involved killing of a driver was documented in 2020. Four of the five killings fell within a single seven-day window, December 11 to 17, at the height of the pandemic-era surge in gun violence.

Corrections to Circulating Lists

Versions of this victim list circulate publicly, in Gig Workers Rising's database and in viral social media compilations. Every case on this page was verified independently against news reports, police statements, prosecutors' offices, and court records. This section corrects the errors in those circulating versions, and the compilations themselves are not a source for any fact in this dataset.
Errors CorrectedA sixth name circulates under 2020 that does not belong there:
The institutional database carries Michael Wallace, an Uber driver who died after a fight with a passenger in Louisville, under 2020. The database's own cited sources date his death to August 9, 2018. He appears in this project's 2018 dataset, not here, and the 2020 total is five, not six.
Errors CorrectedDate errors in the circulating lists:
Lists date the Farrens killing January 30; he was killed the morning of January 28, and January 30 is the date the arrest was announced. Lists date the Mseer shooting December 12; the FBI and the federal indictment place it shortly before midnight on December 11, and he died in the hospital days later. Lists date the Thomas killing December 17; that is the date he died, and he was shot on December 15.
Errors CorrectedLocation error:
Lists place the Farrens killing in "Chester County, VA." No such county exists in Virginia. Chester is the unincorporated Chesterfield County community where the victim lived; the killing occurred 25 miles away on Providence Creek Road, also in Chesterfield County.
Partially SupportedCompilations assign suspect races that the public record does not document.
This dataset codes suspect race only where publicly documented via booking photo, court imagery, or law enforcement description. None of the seven murder-charged individuals in 2020 carries such a coding: not Bernard E. Smith, not the four federal defendants in the Mseer case, and not Wade or Bebic. Any compilation that assigns a race to any of these seven is asserting a fact not in the public record. Race lookups for all seven are queued for a future revision.
Status UpdatesCompilation case statuses are frozen at compile time.
Since the circulating lists were compiled: Wade pleaded guilty to second-degree murder (March 2023) and was sentenced to 18 years 4 months (June 2023), and Bebic was sentenced to 9 years 6 months for first-degree manslaughter (June 2023); a divided federal appeals panel ruled in the family's wrongful-death case that Uber owed its Washington drivers a duty of care (2024); and four Hoover Criminal Gang members were federally indicted for the Mseer killing (March 2024), converting a case every circulating list still carries as unsolved into a charged one, with federal death-penalty-eligible counts.
Methodology: Victim list anchored to Gig Workers Rising's Murdered Behind the Wheel report and public database, the institutional list covering 2017 through April 2023, independently verified and expanded; every fact on this page cites local news, police statements, prosecutors' offices, or court records. Claims examined in the corrections section above come from that database and from viral social media compilations; the compilations are not cited as a source for any fact. Inclusion criteria: homicide of a driver working for an app-based rideshare or delivery platform (Amazon Flex included) at the time of the killing, or killed in direct connection with that work. Killings while working but not because of the job are included motive-blind with circumstances stated, matching the institutional list's murdered-while-working standard; the Mseer case (mistaken for a gang rival while arriving for a pickup) and the Smith case (caught in gunfire aimed at others mid-delivery) are included on that basis. Non-criminal traffic accidents are excluded. No police-involved killing of a driver was documented in 2020. Bias-motive coding is omitted for this dataset. Suspect race comes from a booking, prison or court record, a published photo, or a news report, and the Race source column shows which; where the author judged race from a photo without a record it reads “Author call,” surnames are never treated as documentation, and anyone whose race could not be verified is left unverified. Relationship between killer and victim is coded documented-only: Passenger (rode on or summoned the fatal trip), Customer (delivery recipient), Stranger with the mechanism noted, Known dispute, or Ex-partner where applicable; where a suspect is charged but the relationship is unreported it is coded Not documented, and unsolved cases always code Unknown regardless of any suspected mechanism. "Solved/Charged" counts any case with at least one murder-charged or murder-arrested suspect. All dates, ages, sentences, and statuses verified as of August 2026.
