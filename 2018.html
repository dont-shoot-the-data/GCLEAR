Corrections to Circulating Lists

Versions of this victim list circulate publicly, in Gig Workers Rising's database and in viral social media compilations. Every case on this page was verified independently against news reports, police statements, prosecutors' offices, and court records. This section corrects the errors in those circulating versions, and the circulating versions are not a source for any fact in this dataset.
Errors CorrectedThe circulating 2018 count is 4; the correct count is 3.
The institutional database lists Mourice Foster, a Cleveland Lyft driver, under August 28, 2018, but its own cited source is 2017 coverage. Contemporaneous Cleveland reporting, the Associated Press wire, and the medical examiner all date the killing to shortly after midnight on Monday, August 28, 2017 (August 28, 2018 fell on a Tuesday). The Foster case belongs on the 2017 page, and 2018's verified total is 3.
Errors CorrectedYear and date errors in the circulating lists:
The institutional database dates the Wallace killing August 9, 2020, even though its own cited sources are 2018 news coverage. The fare dispute and beating occurred August 4, 2018, and Wallace died August 7; August 9 is the date coverage broke. The same database's memorial list of drivers killed since February 2022 also includes DaJuan Jenkins, who was killed in February 2018.
Partially SupportedCompilations attach a race to every suspect by default.
This dataset codes suspect race only where publicly documented. 2018 has exactly one murder-charged individual, Anderson, and no located outlet states his race in text; it is coded not documented pending a manual photo verification. The Jenkins killers were never identified, and the passenger in the Wallace case was never named. The 2018 record documents the race of no killer at all, so no racial claim about the year's suspects, in any direction, is supported.
Status UpdatesCompilation case statuses are frozen at compile time.
Circulating lists carry the Kirilov suspect as a cab driver ruled incompetent, the case's posture when they were compiled. Since then, Anderson was tried, convicted by a jury of first-degree premeditated murder on March 26, 2026, and immediately sentenced by Judge William Stone to life in the Florida Department of Corrections, nearly eight years after the killing.
Methodology: Victim list anchored to Gig Workers Rising's Murdered Behind the Wheel report and public database, independently verified and expanded; every fact above cites local news, police statements, prosecutors' offices, or court records. Claims examined in the corrections section come from that database and from viral social media compilations; the compilations are not cited as a source for any fact. Inclusion criteria: homicide of a driver working for an app-based rideshare or delivery platform (Amazon Flex included) at the time of the killing, or killed in direct connection with that work. Killings while working but not because of the job are included motive-blind with circumstances stated, matching the institutional list's murdered-while-working standard. Non-criminal traffic accidents are excluded. No police-involved killing of a driver was documented in 2018. Bias-motive coding is omitted for this dataset. Suspect race is recorded only where publicly documented via booking photo, court imagery, or law enforcement description; unknowns are coded as unknown, never inferred, and neither surnames nor complexion descriptions are treated as documentation. 2018 predates the compiled lists this project cross-checks, so race codings for this year are textual-only or absent; no located coverage states a race in text for any 2018 victim or suspect, and no provisional codings appear on this page. Relationship between killer and victim is coded documented-only: Passenger (rode on or summoned the fatal trip), Customer (delivery recipient), Stranger with the mechanism noted, Known dispute, or Ex-partner where applicable; unsolved cases always code Unknown regardless of any suspected mechanism, which is why the Jenkins case codes Unknown despite his family's public statement that the ride request was a setup. "Solved/Charged" counts any case with at least one murder-charged or murder-arrested suspect. The Wallace case carries its own status: the passenger who fought him was identified by police at the scene, the death was investigated pending an autopsy whose findings were never publicly reported, no charges were ever filed, and the case is counted in neither the solved/charged nor the unsolved figures, the same treatment as the identified-but-uncharged 2024 Wilmot case. The cause of death in the Wallace case was never publicly ruled; it is recorded as the beating described by police and family, with that limitation stated in the table. All dates, ages, sentences, and statuses verified as of August 2026.
