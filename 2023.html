Corrections to Circulating Lists

Versions of this victim list circulate publicly, in Gig Workers Rising's database and in viral social media compilations. Every case on this page was verified independently against news reports, prosecutors' offices, and court records; this section corrects the errors in those circulating versions.
Errors CorrectedDate errors in the circulating lists:
The Gig Workers Rising database dates the Bardales Valle killing January 8; New Orleans police records place it March 8. Viral social media compilations date the Diaz killing March 16 (the arrest warrant says March 9), the Cooke killing April 24 (he was killed April 19), the Cole disappearance May 11 (he vanished mid-shift April 28), and the Kamara crash March 14 (it occurred March 15). One working list dated the Grund killing October 19; the Jacksonville Sheriff's Office places it October 6.
FalseClaim: Johnell Hampton was the suspect in the Stiller case.
A viral compilation lists Hampton as the suspect. Hampton, 21, was Andrew Stiller's rideshare passenger, the apparent target of the ambush, and was murdered alongside him. The shooter convicted at an April 2025 trial is Tyree Quinn.
Errors CorrectedRace and platform codings corrected:
Compilations code Phoebe Copas (the Piedra Garcia suspect) as Hispanic; no source states her race in writing; see her row for how she is coded. Compilations code Adriana Arocha-Duque as White; she was a Venezuelan asylum seeker. Milton Pillacela Ayora is widely listed as an Uber driver; Lyft's own statement and Chicago reporting identify the fatal trip as a Lyft ride, though a police report obtained by Gig Workers Rising listed Uber.
Partially SupportedCompilations code at-large, unnamed, and juvenile suspects as Black by default. This dataset codes suspect race only where it can be verified. Of the 27 counted suspects in 2023, 24 have a verified race: 21 Black, 2 Hispanic, 1 White. The other 3 are left unverified rather than assigned a race.
Methodology: Victim list compiled from Gig Workers Rising's Murdered Behind the Wheel report and public database (which end in April 2023), supplemented by independent discovery sweeps for May through December 2023, then verified case by case against local news, prosecutors' offices, and court records; the lead lists are not cited as a source for any fact and their errors are corrected above. Inclusion criteria: homicide of a driver working for an app-based rideshare or delivery platform (Amazon Flex included) at the time of the killing, or killed in direct connection with that work. Killings while working but not because of the job (stray bullets, random attacks, crossfire) are included motive-blind with circumstances stated, matching the institutional list's murdered-while-working standard. Vehicle deaths are excluded even when a driver was charged, because this study counts intentional killings only; the Kamara and Kelleher cases are excluded on that basis. The police-involved killing (Henderson) is counted in victim totals and broken out of homicide and suspect statistics. Bias-motive coding is omitted for this dataset. Suspect race comes from a booking, prison or court record, a published photo, or a news report, and the Race source column shows which; where the author judged race from a photo without a record it reads “Author call,” surnames are never treated as documentation, and anyone whose race could not be verified is left unverified. Relationship between killer and victim is coded documented-only: Passenger (rode on the fatal trip), Customer (delivery recipient), Stranger with the mechanism noted, Known dispute, or Ex-partner where applicable; where a suspect is charged but the relationship is unreported it is coded Not documented, and unsolved cases always code Unknown regardless of any suspected mechanism. "Solved/Charged" counts any case with at least one murder-charged or murder-arrested suspect; the Piedra Garcia case is counted there and closed, as the charged suspect died before trial. Juvenile and withheld-name defendants in the Orozco, Ahmad Yar, and Djembissi-adjacent matters reflect identity-confidentiality practices. All dates, ages, sentences, and statuses verified as of July 2026.
